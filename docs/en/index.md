## 👶🏻 Tech Interview Encyclopedia for Junior Developers 📖

##### [Made by] : 1mo

This is a personal tech wiki that combines what I studied in the [Codyssey AI/SW All-in-One program](https://usr.codyssey.kr) with core CS topics that often come up in junior developer interviews.

- **What I learned at Codyssey**: concepts I needed while building missions myself, such as a portfolio site, Mini Redis, Mini Git, an SNS database design, AWS infrastructure, and e-commerce data analysis
- **Interview staples**: web (browsers, HTTP, authentication, security), data structures, algorithms, computer architecture, operating systems, databases, and Git, studied further and organized here

Every article is written so that readers without a CS degree can follow along. The English pages are translations of the original Korean articles.

✔️ These are my own notes, so some information may be wrong. If you find a mistake, please let me know through a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/dubu-alt/Codyssey-B1/issues)!
