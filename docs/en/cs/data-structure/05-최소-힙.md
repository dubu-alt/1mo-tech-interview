**Heap** = a tree structure that keeps the rule "a parent is always smaller than its children (min heap)."
It is represented as an array: the children of the parent at index i are at `2i+1` and `2i+2`.

```
        (1)          ← root = always the minimum
       /   \
     (3)   (2)
     / \
   (7) (5)
```

- **peek**: see the minimum → just look at the root → O(1)
- **push**: add at the end, then move it up while comparing with its parent (`_heapify_up`) → O(log n)
- **pop**: take out the root, move the last element to the root, then move it down while comparing with its children (`_heapify_down`) → O(log n)

## Why Is a Heap a Good Fit for TTL Management?

TTL stores `(expire_at, key)` and has to keep asking **which one expires first**.
- The one with the smallest expiration time = the minimum → it is always at the root of the heap!
- This is far more efficient than sorting or scanning everything each time.

## Summary

| Concept | Plain-language summary |
|------|-------------------|
| Min heap | A pyramid where the smallest/most urgent item is always on top |
